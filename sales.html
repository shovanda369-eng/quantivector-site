How It Works

Every Signal, Fully Explained

Here's exactly what QuantiVector draws on your chart — and how each piece helps you study the setup.

1Setup Plan

Every Signal Comes With Reference Levels

The moment a signal fires, QuantiVector auto-draws Entry, Stop Loss, and three Take-Profit reference levels right on the chart — plus a shaded Setup Zone showing exactly where price reacted. No manual drawing and no lines to plot yourself — every level is calculated for you, for study purposes.

2Backtest Data

What the Backtest Panel Shows

A live Backtest Report panel sits right on your chart showing historical win rate, qualified trades, and the average score of winning setups — so you can see how this exact pair and timeframe have behaved historically. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.

3Market Structure

See Market Structure Without the Guesswork

QuantiVector automatically marks Higher Highs, Higher Lows, Lower Highs, and Lower Lows — plus every Break of Structure — so you can see whether the trend is intact or shifting, without staring at candles for hours.

4Timeframe Bias

Check Every Timeframe In One Glance

The Multi-Timeframe Bias table reads 5m through 1D at the same time and shows whether each is Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral, so you can see whether a setup lines up with the bigger trend.

5Trap Detection

Spot Fakeouts With Trap Zone Detection

Before a real move begins, price often fakes traders out. QuantiVector flags these Trap Zones automatically alongside the Buy/Sell Setup Zone — fully customizable in settings — as a study aid for recognizing these patterns yourself.

Proof, Not Promises

A live scoreboard, right on your chart

No cherry-picked backtest screenshots. QuantiVector keeps a running, self-updating historical record for the symbol you're studying — for your own research. Historical performance is not a guarantee or prediction of future results.

Questions, answered

No. Every signal is calculated from already-closed candle data, so historical BUY/SELL signals never move or disappear after the fact.

Can I see exactly how the trend or score is calculated?

The core detection and scoring logic is kept internal to protect the strategy — you get full control over every visual, label, color, and alert instead.

Which markets and timeframes does it work on?

QuantiVector runs on any symbol and timeframe available on TradingView — crypto, forex, indices, futures, and stocks.

Do I need other indicators alongside it?

No. Signal detection, chart-level marking, confluence scoring, multi-timeframe bias, and backtest tracking are all built in. Extra tools like VWAP and order blocks are optional add-ons.
