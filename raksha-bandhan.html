How It Works

Every Signal, Fully Explained

Here's exactly what QuantiVector draws on your chart — and how each piece helps you trade with more confidence.

1

Every Trade Comes With a Full Plan

The moment a signal fires, QuantiVector auto-draws your Entry, Stop Loss, and three Take-Profit levels right on the chart — plus a shaded Setup Zone showing exactly where price reacted. No manual drawing, no guesswork, no lines to plot yourself.

2

Know the Odds Before You Trade

A live Backtest Report panel sits right on your chart showing win rate, qualified trades, and the average score of winning setups — so you know how this exact pair and timeframe have performed before you risk a single rupee.

3

See Market Structure Without the Guesswork

QuantiVector automatically marks Higher Highs, Higher Lows, Lower Highs, and Lower Lows — plus every Break of Structure — so you always know whether the trend is intact or shifting, without staring at candles for hours.

4

Check Every Timeframe In One Glance

The Multi-Timeframe Bias table reads 5m through 1D at the same time and tells you if each is Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral — so you never take a trade against the bigger trend again.

5

Avoid Fakeouts With Trap Zone Detection

Before a real move begins, price often fakes traders out. QuantiVector flags these Trap Zones automatically alongside the Buy/Sell Setup Zone — fully customizable in settings — so you don't get shaken out of good trades.
