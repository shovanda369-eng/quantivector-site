01 · Core Signal Engine Top Feature — Automated Signals

A trend-continuation engine that waits for proof

QuantiVector continuously reads whether the market is trending up or down, then watches for the exact moment a reaction against that trend resolves back in its favor — before it ever marks a signal.

  • Setup Zone — every candle in a reaction move is grouped into one clean shaded box, not flagged individually.
  • Confirmed BUY / SELL — a large, unmistakable label fires automatically only on the breakout candle, in the trend's direction.
  • One trade at a time — no new signal fires until the previous one has fully resolved.
  • Non-repainting — built entirely on closed-candle data, so history never moves.

Show/hide zones, labels, and text independently for BUY and SELL — every string is fully editable for your own branding.

01b · Chop Protection Top Feature — Automatic Trap Zone ID

Automatically flags the chop that wrecks accounts

A separate detection layer watches for price caught between two internal trend references — the classic condition where trend-following signals fail. QuantiVector spots it and mutes new signals automatically, without you lifting a finger.

  • No new BUY or SELL signal can fire while price is inside a Trap Zone
  • The zone is shaded live on the chart so you can see exactly why it's quiet
  • Optional alerts fire the moment price enters or exits a Trap Zone

Show/hide the zone box and its text independently, with fully custom Trap Zone label text and color.

Choppy price gets boxed and muted — the next real breakout still fires clean

02 · Trade Plan Top Feature — Fibonacci TP/SL & Auto Trade Zone

A complete Entry / Stop Loss / Take-Profit plan, drawn instantly

The moment a signal confirms, QuantiVector plots your entire risk plan — no manual measuring required.

  • Entry — the confirmed signal's closing price.
  • Stop Loss — set at the extreme of the setup zone that led to the signal.
  • TP1 / TP2 / TP3 — three progressively farther profit targets projected from the Entry/SL relationship.
  • Lines grow live — each level extends bar-by-bar with the chart for a natural, developing-trade look.
  • Freezes on resolution — lines stop the moment SL is closed beyond, or TP3 is touched — and stay as chart history.

Toggle each level and zone fill independently, full color control, plus object-cleanup limits to protect chart performance over long histories.

03 · Signal Strength Top Feature — Auto Signal Strength

An AI-style confidence score on every signal

Not every confirmed signal is equally strong. QuantiVector blends eight independent market-condition checks into one clean percentage — displayed right next to the Stop Loss line.

  • Multi-timeframe trend agreement across 1H, 4H, and 1D
  • Reaction depth and volume character read
  • Trend strength, momentum, and closing conviction
  • A liquidity-sweep check for smart-money-style stop hunts
  • The indicator's own real, observed win rate — separately for BUY and SELL

The internal weighting formula stays private — only the final, color-coded percentage and label size are exposed as settings.

04 · Live Performance Top Feature — Win Rate on Any Symbol

A self-updating backtest, always on your chart

No external backtesting tool needed. Drop QuantiVector on any stock, coin, index, or pair and it automatically calculates that symbol's own real win rate, live — updating as new signals resolve.

  • Win Rate — calculated automatically for the exact chart you're on, among signals that meet the current quality bar.
  • Qualified Trades — total count that cleared the bar.
  • Avg Score – Wins — the average confidence score behind winning trades.
  • Self-adjusting bar — the "qualified" threshold automatically recalibrates toward real market behavior over time, instead of sitting at a fixed cutoff.

Position the panel in any corner, and fully restyle text size, header, row backgrounds, and win/loss/rate colors.

Live report panel shown top-right, with all four corner positions available

05 · Context Table

Top-down bias across six timeframes, at a glance

A compact table shows the trend bias on 5m, 15m, 30m, 1H, 4H, and 1D simultaneously — each labeled Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral.

  • Purely informational — never overrides or blocks a signal
  • Color-coded green / red / gray for instant reading
  • Feeds directly into the confidence score's HTF-alignment factor

Single show/hide toggle — placed top-right by default.

06 · Independent Add-Ons

Five extra tools, fully optional

Every tool below is completely independent of the signal engine, trade plan, and confidence score — switch any of them on without changing your trading logic. All are off by default.

VWAP

Session Volume-Weighted Average Price plotted directly on the chart, with a customizable line color.

Extra EMA Lines

Two fully independent EMAs — each with its own show/hide, length, color, and width.

Supply / Demand Zones

Auto-marked at pivot highs/lows, growing until invalidated, then frozen. Shows only the most recent N zones per side.

Market Structure (BOS / CHoCH)

Automatic HH / LH / HL / LL swing labels, plus Break-of-Structure and Change-of-Character detection with connecting lines.

Order Block Finder

Marks the last opposite-colored candle before a strong, structure-breaking impulse move. Valid blocks stay live and keep growing; invalidated blocks recolor and relabel instead of disappearing, so you can see the full history of live and mitigated zones — with adjustable impulse strength, lookback, and per-side block counts.

Alerts can be enabled or disabled globally, with fully custom messages.

08 · Design Philosophy

Hidden methodology, visible results

Core calculation logic — trend detection, confidence-score weighting — is intentionally not exposed as a setting, protecting the strategy while giving you total control over how it looks.

  • Custom text for every label — Buy, Sell, Setup Zone, Trap Zone
  • Full color control across zones, lines, and panels
  • Clean chart legend — only "QuantiVector" shows in the status line
  • Self-trimming object pools keep performance stable across long chart histories
