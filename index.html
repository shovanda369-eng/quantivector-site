    Does QuantiVector repaint?

    No. Every signal is calculated from already-closed candle data, so historical BUY/SELL signals never move or disappear after the fact.

    Can I see exactly how the trend or score is calculated?

    The core detection and scoring logic is kept internal to protect the strategy — you get full control over every visual, label, color, and alert instead.

    Which markets and timeframes does it work on?

    QuantiVector runs on any symbol and timeframe available on TradingView — crypto, forex, indices, futures, and stocks.

    Do I need other indicators alongside it?

    No. Signal detection, trade planning, confidence scoring, multi-timeframe bias, and backtest tracking are all built in. Extra tools like VWAP and order blocks are optional add-ons.

    Can I rebrand the labels and colors?

    Yes — every label's text, every zone color, and every panel's appearance is fully customizable.
