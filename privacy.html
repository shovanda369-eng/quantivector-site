Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

QuantiVector is a TradingView indicator product operated by Nestail (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or purchase QuantiVector, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout or support: name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Your TradingView username, which we use to grant you indicator access.
  • Payment and transaction details processed by our payment partner, Cashfree. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details — these are handled directly by Cashfree.
  • Usage and device data collected automatically via cookies and similar technologies, including through Meta Pixel / Conversions API and Google Ads / Analytics, to understand how visitors use this site and to measure ad performance.

How we use your information

  • To activate and deliver your TradingView indicator access.
  • To process payments and send order confirmations.
  • To respond to support queries sent via email or WhatsApp.
  • To send you updates about your subscription or relevant offers.
  • To measure and improve the performance of this website and our advertising, using aggregated or pseudonymised data.

Sharing your information

We share information only with the service providers needed to run QuantiVector: Cashfree (payment processing), and Meta and Google (advertising and analytics platforms, used in line with their own privacy policies). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and pixel-based tracking (including Meta Pixel and Google Ads tracking) to measure traffic and ad performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

Data retention and security

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide your subscription and comply with our legal and accounting obligations, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.
